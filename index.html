Why host with NWSE

NorthWest Student Exchange has offered quality student exchange programs since 1987. We give every student and host family individual attention, so you always have someone to turn to.

More about us
  • A trusted non-profitA U.S. Department of State designated exchange program since 1987, listed with the Council on Standards for International Educational Travel (CSIET).
  • Careful matchingWe get to know every student through interviews, references, essays and hobbies, so your family and your student are a good fit.
  • Support close to homeOur local coordinators have lived or studied abroad, and many have hosted exchange students themselves.
  • Everything arrangedWe provide orientations and coordinate insurance, travel and visas for every student.

All you need: an open home

You don’t have to have children. You don’t have to travel.

You simply need to be willing to open your home, share your daily life and learn from another culture.

Open the door to international friendships that last a lifetime. Share your culture, get to know a student from another country and experience the world without leaving home.
